Privacy notice.

This local starter stores service requests on the device or server where it is run. Configure secure hosting and a privacy policy that reflects the real business before public use.

What is collected

A booking may include your name, phone, vehicle, issue, address, preferred time and an optional photo. This information is used to review and manage your request.

Who can see it

Booking information is not displayed publicly. The owner dashboard requires an admin PIN. Anyone with access to the device, its files or the local network may still be able to affect data security; use a strong PIN and trusted networks.

Retention and contact

Bookings are stored in a local JSON file until the owner removes or archives the data. Add verified contact and retention information before the site is made public.
